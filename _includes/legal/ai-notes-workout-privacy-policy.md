Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spam Musubi Technologies LLC handles information when You use Workout.

Workout is designed to help You capture gym notes quickly, parse them into structured workout history, track progress, and optionally sync saved workouts with Apple services that You enable.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

You may provide information when You use or contact Us about the Service, including:

- Workout notes, imported notes, session titles, exercise names, set details, weights, repetitions, RPE, RIR, distance, duration, session notes, and similar training information You enter, import, dictate, edit, or save.
- Custom exercise names, learned aliases, parser corrections, onboarding choices, training goals, reminder settings, display preferences, and other app settings.
- Support messages, email address, and other contact details if You contact Us.
- Purchase-related choices, such as which Subscription or In-app Purchase You select, as processed through the Application Store.

### Workout, Health, and Progress Information

Workout may process information on Your Device to provide logging and progress features, including:

- Raw workout text and imported text files You choose to provide.
- Parsed exercises, sets, weights, units, reps, notes, muscle groups, personal records, trends, charts, and workout history.
- Training reminder settings and local notification preferences.
- CSV files You choose to import or export.
- Apple Health workout records if You enable Apple Health sync.

The workout parser is designed to run on Your Device. We do not operate a separate server that receives Your raw workout notes for parsing.

### Device Permissions

The Application may request permissions from Your Device, including:

- **Microphone and Speech Recognition:** If You enable voice input, the Application uses the microphone and Apple speech recognition features to turn spoken workout notes into text. The Application does not intentionally save audio recordings after dictation ends.
- **Apple Health:** If You enable Apple Health sync, the Application may request permission to read and write workout records. Saved workouts may include workout date, estimated duration, activity type, a Workout session identifier, and raw workout note text in Apple Health workout metadata.
- **Notifications:** If You enable reminders, the Application can schedule local training reminders on Your Device.
- **iCloud:** Workout uses Core Data with CloudKit to mirror app data through Your private iCloud account when iCloud is available for the Application and enabled on Your Device.

Permission choices are controlled through Your Device settings.

### Usage Data, Diagnostics, and Analytics

The Application may collect limited Usage Data and diagnostics to understand, maintain, and improve the Service. This may include app events, feature usage, crash information, performance data, Device model, operating system version, region, purchase entitlement status, and similar technical information.

The Application includes an analytics integration that only runs when configured by the Company. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the current iOS build.

Apple may provide developers with App Store analytics, crash reports, performance diagnostics, purchase records, and similar information according to Your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

### Payment Information

If You make an In-app Purchase or purchase a Subscription, payment is processed by the Application Store. We do not receive or store Your full payment card number, billing card security code, or full Application Store account credentials.

We may receive limited purchase, receipt, device identifier, attribution, or entitlement information needed to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent fraud, analyze purchase performance, or provide support.

## How Information Is Stored

Workout history, notes, custom exercises, parser memory, and settings are primarily stored on Your Device.

Depending on Your Device settings, Apple account settings, backup settings, and Application configuration, some Application data may be included in iCloud backups or mirrored through Your private iCloud account using CloudKit. We do not operate a separate cloud workout database for Your workout history.

Exported CSV files are created on Your Device and shared only when You choose to share them.
