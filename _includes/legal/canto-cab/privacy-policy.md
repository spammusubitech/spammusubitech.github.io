The current version of Canto Cab:

- Does not require an account or login.
- Does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party product analytics.

## Information We Handle

### Local Game Progress and Settings

Canto Cab may save the following information locally on Your Device:

- Selected and unlocked levels, completed levels, and related progression state.
- Gameplay preferences and settings, including music, sound-effect, and voice volume.
- Haptic-feedback preference.
- Other local state needed to resume or operate the game.

This information is used to operate the game and remember Your choices. It is not associated with a Canto Cab account because the current version does not provide accounts.

Depending on Your Device, operating system, Application Store, and backup settings, local Application data may be included in a device backup or transferred to a replacement Device by Apple, Google, or another platform provider. We do not operate a separate cloud database for Your game progress.

### Support Communications

If You contact Us, You may provide information such as Your name, email address, Device type, operating system version, screenshots, gameplay details, and the contents of Your message. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, maintain business records, and improve the Service.

We may receive limited purchase or entitlement information needed to provide or restore paid content, answer support requests, comply with law, or prevent fraud.

The current version does not use a separate third-party purchase-management SDK. If that changes, We will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate before using the new provider to process information.
